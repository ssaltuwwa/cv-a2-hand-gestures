Privacy, consent and synthetic media

Privacy. The model runs in your browser with ONNX Runtime Web. Photos and webcam frames are processed on your device and are never uploaded or stored.

Consent. All people in our training photos are team members who agreed to be photographed for this course project.

Synthetic media. The 36 clips in the Synthetic clips tab are AI-generated and labelled SYNTHETIC on every frame.

Limitations. Trained on 644 photos of three people's hands, mostly indoors. Accuracy drops for unusual poses, backlight and very low resolution (see the stress test). Not for safety-critical or surveillance use.
